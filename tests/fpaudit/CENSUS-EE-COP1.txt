EE COP1 census 1: 16 ops x 400 cases x {default, soft, accurate}
================================================================
Rig: EE audit exports with harness-thread fault-filter registration
(the thread-affinity blocker resolved to one RegisterFaultHandlerThread
call -- the fault machinery services registered threads regardless of
which one). Scratch block at 16MB through the memory system, actual
recompiled block vs bounded-stepped interpreter (ps2f-based oracle,
confirmed by the FMAX known-answer split: rec clamp 7f7fffff vs
oracle saturation 7fffffff). Columns: fd /400, ACC /400, FCR31 /400.

Findings:
- The two options are COMPLEMENTARY, not overlapping: soft float
  closes the multiply/divide/sqrt/rsqrt/fused family (values and
  flags) but does not route add/sub/abs/neg; accurate arithmetic
  closes add/sub (and mostly mul/fused values) but leaves the
  divide family untouched. Neither alone blankets the FPU; both
  together would, except for the orphan below.
- ORPHAN DIVERGENCE, uncovered by every option: ABS.S and NEG.S.
  Hardware treats them as pure sign-bit operations; the oracle
  passes bit patterns through. The recompiler clamps their results
  (denormal -> 0, exp-255 -> FLT_MAX), destroying round-trippable
  bit patterns. Candidate fix: exempt ABS/NEG from result clamping
  as pure bitops -- with the recorded caveat that the EE clamp
  discipline may rely on those clamps to keep exp-255 out of
  downstream host arithmetic, so the change needs the interaction
  audit (which ops input-clamp) before it lands, same gates as the
  VU clamp elision.
- MAX/MIN/MOV/CVT.W bit-exact in every config, mirroring the VU
  comparison-family result.
- Default-mode scales mirror the VU: add/sub at the small clamp/
  special class, divide family largest, flags concentrated on
  multiply/divide (the VU's O/U sticky story again -- the class
  gate precedent applies if promotion is ever wanted here).
